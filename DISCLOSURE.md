# Public vs internal

The public channel and internal production are distinct piles. This page says which is which. It is not a request form and not an office.

## Public SITREP

Versioned files in this repo. Perishability on page one. Scoring ledger. Public Addendum. CC BY 4.0.

## Internal production

Drafts, model-vs-model fights, unpublished sources, disposition notes, rejected findings with names. Not this public tree.

## Redacted disclosure

Internal process may be disclosed, with appropriate redaction, upon a reasonable written request that names a specified purpose. That follows HASEOS-IDAO Article XIV (reserved, not stood up). Secrets, keeper material, certs, and ports are never disclosed.

Steward (HITL) ships public files. Models draft and red-team. They do not publish.

Silence carries no signal. That is a public rule, not proof the internal pile is empty.

A SITREP is not the Constitution, not DSM, not the Trust Seal.
